Our Vision & Mandate

Strategic Vision

To function as an international standard hub for teaching and comparative research integrating statutory law systems with Islamic legal philosophies.

Departmental Mission

Empower students through comprehensive critical reasoning, analytical skill acquisition, and hands-on professional litigation training inside our state-of-the-art Moot Court.

Offered Degree Programs

World-class syllabus matching bar certification requirements and Shariah criteria.

Undergraduate

LLB (Hons) in Al-Fiqh & Law

A standard 4-year curriculum spanning constitutional law, Islamic jurisprudence, civil/criminal procedure, and corporate policy.

Graduate

LLM in Comparative Jurisprudence

1-Year advanced research focus emphasizing legal analysis, human rights treaties, international finance laws, and Islamic civil codes.

Research

M.Phil & PhD Programs

Advanced academic tracks focusing on doctoral dissertations in modern constitutional reforms and classic Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali theories.
